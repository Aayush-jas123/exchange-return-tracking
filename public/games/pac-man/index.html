Pac-Man

How to play ▼

Goal: Eat all pellets in the maze before the ghosts catch you.

  • Small dots = 10 points
  • Large dots = 50 points + frighten ghosts
  • Eat frightened ghosts = 200-1600 pts (combo)
  • 3 lives — lose one per ghost collision
  • More ghosts appear over time — stay sharp!
  • Online: Shared maze, highest score wins

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD on desktop. D-pad or swipe on mobile.
