Crossy Road

How to play ▼

Objective: Cross as many lanes as possible without getting hit by vehicles.

Controls — PC: Arrow keys or WASD to hop forward, backward, left, right.

Controls — Mobile: Use the D-pad buttons or swipe on the screen.

Scoring: Each lane crossed forward = 1 point. Trees and billboards block your path — plan your route. Traffic gets denser and faster the further you go. Standing on grass for more than 5 seconds ends the run.
