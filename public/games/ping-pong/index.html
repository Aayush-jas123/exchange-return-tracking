How to play ▼

Objective: First to 10 points wins. But watch out — miss 3 rallies in a row and you lose instantly!

Speed: The ball gets faster with every hit. Longer games = faster rallies.

Controls — PC: W / S keys to move your paddle

Controls — Mobile: Touch and drag to move your paddle.
