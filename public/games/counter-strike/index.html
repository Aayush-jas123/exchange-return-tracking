How to play ▼

Objective: You are a Counter-Terrorist. Eliminate every bot before the round timer expires. Each round spawns more enemies. Your total kills before elimination is your score.

PC Controls: WASD to move, Mouse to aim, Left click to shoot, Right click to aim down sights, Space to jump, Shift to walk, C/Ctrl to crouch, R to reload, 1/2/3 to switch weapons.

Mobile Controls: Left joystick to move, right side of screen to aim, fire button to shoot, weapon switch buttons for guns, jump/crouch buttons for movement.

Scoring: Every kill counts. Headshots are one-hit kills. Walk or crouch for tighter accuracy. The deeper you push into rounds, the tougher the bots become.
