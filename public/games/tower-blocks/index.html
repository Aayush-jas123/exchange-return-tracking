Tower Blocks

How to play ▼

Objective: Stack the tower as high as you can. The block slides across the top of the tower — drop it and whatever hangs over the edge is sliced away.

Controls — PC: Click, or press the spacebar, to drop the moving block.

Controls — Mobile: Tap anywhere on the board to drop.

Scoring: 1 point per block placed. A near-perfect drop snaps back to full size and keeps the tower wide. Miss the tower completely and the run ends.
